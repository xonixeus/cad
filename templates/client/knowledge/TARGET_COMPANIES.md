# Target Companies

## Purpose

A prioritized list of companies where the client is particularly interested in working. Sherlock investigates every company on every scheduled search cycle (Active Search and Selective), independently of broader market research.

## Search Requirements

Default: search each company's official careers site with relevant titles, variations and responsibilities. Add a subsection for any company that needs a special scope (for example, a business unit inside a larger parent company), and say exactly what to search and how to report it.

## Priority Definitions

**High:** Check every scheduled search cycle and prioritize investigation of qualified positions.

**Standard:** Check every scheduled search cycle, with normal investigation and reporting priority.

Priority affects research order and attention, not qualification standards. A High-priority employer does not make an unsuitable job worth pursuing.

## Reporting

Sherlock distinguishes opportunities found at target companies from those found through broader research, and identifies target-company opportunities clearly in reports to Maestro. The absence of qualified openings is recorded without inventing results or repeating unchanged findings. Do not double-count postings or claim full coverage when only one source was checked.

## Maintenance

Only the client adds, removes or reprioritizes companies. Sherlock may recommend companies to Maestro.
