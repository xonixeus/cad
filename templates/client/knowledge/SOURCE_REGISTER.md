## Evidence conventions

- Career facts and their classifications live in `CLAIMS_LEDGER.md`. Each ledger row names its sources from this register, or "Client" for the client's direct statements.
- A resume can show that a claim appears in source material; it cannot by itself establish that the claim is accurate. Repetition across resumes does not verify a claim.
- Job titles: the ledger's employment record holds official titles, approved alternatives, and the client's confirmations.
- Job postings kept for reference (prefix `J`) are never evidence about the client. Do not infer application status, preferences or vacancy status from them.
