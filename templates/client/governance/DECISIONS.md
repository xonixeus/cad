# Client Decisions Log

Decisions the client made about how the department works for them. Newest first. Maestro records each decision with its date and source (issue or comment link); agents follow the latest entry. Department-wide rules are in `cad:TEAM.md`; record here only what the client chose.

Examples of what belongs here: the operating mode, base resumes approved, services the client declined, report cadence changes, exceptions to a default rule.
