# Standing Delegation: Resume Approval (APPROVED_FOR_USE)

**Status: NOT GRANTED.** Until the client grants it and Maestro records the grant below, every resume needs the client's own approval (`cad:TEAM.md` §11). Delete this file if the client never wants a delegation.

Granted by: _(client, with the issue or comment link where they granted it)_
Granted on: _(date)_
Recorded by: Maestro, _(issue link)_

## What the client delegates

After Scribe drafts a resume, Maestro reviews it against every requirement and either returns it to Scribe with instructions or marks it `APPROVED_FOR_USE`. Maestro brings open questions to the client.

## Conditions Maestro applies before setting APPROVED_FOR_USE

1. All ten steps of Maestro's document acceptance checklist (`cad:agents/maestro/AGENTS.md`, "Accepting Documents") pass.
2. Every material claim (metrics, titles, dates, credentials) maps in Scribe's claim map to a row in `knowledge/CLAIMS_LEDGER.md` that is Verified with Use `Resume`, and respects that row's Limits. A Pending claim cannot be used; it goes to the client as a question.
3. The PDF is produced and validated: page count, page size, embedded fonts, text extraction, and ATS-safe layout.
4. Any gap against the posting is disclosed in the approval record.
5. The approval is recorded on the Multica issue with the filename, version and checks, and the approved resume is committed to `deliverables/resumes/`.

## Unchanged by this delegation

- An approval permits use only. It never permits submitting, uploading, applying, or contacting employers. The client does those.
- Whether to pursue an opportunity is still the client's decision.
- This covers resumes only. Other documents still need the client's approval unless they extend it here.
