# Interview and Offer Preparation

## When to use

- Watson: when the client reports an interview (interview pack) or an offer (negotiation brief).
- A temporary mock-interviewer specialist created by Maestro for one interview.

## Before you start

1. The posting analysis (`job-posting-analysis`), a company brief (`company-research`), and the materials the client submitted (resume and letter from `client:deliverables/` or the issue).
2. `client:knowledge/CLAIMS_LEDGER.md`: talking points use Verified claims only (see `claims-ledger`).
3. The client's owner profile (`profile-alignment`) for stories, culture fit and delivery style.
4. Whatever the client told you about the interview: stage, format, interviewers, length.

## Interview pack

Use `templates/interview-pack.md`.

1. **The role's likely priorities:** what this interviewer at this stage most needs to learn, from the posting and company brief.
2. **Talking points:** three to six themes, each tied to Verified ledger claims (cite IDs) and, where it fits, a profile-alignment match.
3. **Stories:** two to four short situation–task–action–result stories built only on Verified claims; note which questions each one answers.
4. **Likely questions with suggested answers:** role-specific, behavioral, and "about you" questions. Suggested answers are outlines, not scripts, in the client's voice.
5. **Gaps and how to address them honestly:** each material gap from the assessment, with a truthful framing (transferable experience with IDs, learning plan, or a direct acknowledgment). Never suggest overstating.
6. **Questions for the client to ask:** five to eight, specific to the employer and stage, including at least one that tests a concern from the assessment.
7. **Logistics and reminders:** anything the employer asked candidates to prepare.

## Mock interview (specialist)

Ask questions one at a time, wait for the client's answer, and give brief feedback on content, evidence and clarity. Never invent facts about the client or the employer.

## Negotiation brief

Use `templates/offer-brief.md`.

1. **The offer:** every component the client reported (base, bonus, equity, benefits, start date, work arrangement, title, level).
2. **Against the client's preferences:** compensation benchmark, constraints and priorities from `JOB_SEARCH_PREFERENCES.md`.
3. **Market data:** sourced ranges for comparable roles (`research-methods`), each with source and date.
4. **Negotiable terms and options:** what is commonly negotiable here, realistic asks, and trade-offs.
5. **Risks:** anything in the offer or company brief that matters (for example, contract length, recent layoffs).

State facts and trade-offs. The decision, and all contact with the employer, belong to the client.

## Stop and ask

- The interview's stage or format is unknown and would change the pack (ask the client through Maestro).
- The offer details are incomplete.
